To setup the  EDI INVOICE we must test it first, based on the following information.

 

1) DESADV must be sent to production first, then we can proceed with testing the INVOICE.

2) EDI INVOICE is only tested based on firm orders from the last 3 months.

3) NAD+ST, NAD+BY AND RFF+VA segments must match the UD Codes and Addresses file.

4) RFF+AAK in the INVOICE must match the DESADV BGM+351 sent to production.

5) NAD+SE and RFF+VA must match our Supplier database.

6) FII+BF must match our Supplier database.

7) Please confirm with UD Buyers segments:

  • TOD+++INCOTERMS::4
  • LOC+1+:::INCOTERMS LOCATION (CITY AND COUNTRY)

before You sent the test invoice to us. These information should be according to the contract agreement.

Please send the EDI INVOICE with test indicator 1 in segment UNB 0035 and inform us about it, when all of the above points are completed.
